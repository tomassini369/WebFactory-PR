# Audit 008 — Neutral Liquid Glass reading surfaces

Mode: After / audit existing work. Scope: PR #125, portal/POS/kiosk Liquid Glass.

The detailed photograph showed through reading zones, making both theme inks
unstable. Add a local neutral reading layer and solid text roles while retaining
the approved optics, original images, logo, palettes and current layout.

- Purpose: improve legibility of existing content, not introduce a new style.
- Interaction: reuse opacity and blur controls; no duplicate options.
- Accessibility: minimum reading fill, explicit custom color/ink protection,
  decorative pointer-inert layers, forced colors, print, reduced transparency
  and existing reduced motion. Preserve native fields, focus and navigation.
- Boundaries: no operational POS/kiosk, auth, payment, booking or tenant changes.
- Detector: Impeccable 4.1.0 returned 20 existing recommendations, 18 warnings and
  two advisories, exit 2. No automatic global redesign was applied.
- Evidence and limits: see docs/qa/NEUTRAL-LIQUID-GLASS.md and the PR validation
  block. Pixel checks sample defined rendered text zones; no physical-device
  or whole-platform conformance claim is made.
