# Bright Melodic EDM

Fupi, [Melodic EDM Loops](https://opengameart.org/content/melodic-edm-loops), OpenGameArt.org.

The original `brightmelodicedm.wav` is published under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This demonstration repeats the original loop three times, lowers its volume, adds a fade-out, and encodes it as MP3.
