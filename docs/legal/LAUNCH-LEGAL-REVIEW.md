## Owner / legal decisions required before launch

1. Confirm the service provider's legal identity and publicly appropriate business
   contact details. Existing support/billing emails are reused; this is not evidence
   that a specific legal entity or postal address has been verified.
2. Copyright Office DMCA agent designation: identify legal provider and aliases,
   choose agent, provide accurate matching address/contact details, register through
   https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/ and maintain current designation. Have
   counsel assess 17 USC 512 eligibility, repeat-infringer implementation, notices,
   counter-notices, fair use, restoration and court-action deadlines. A public page
   alone does not establish safe-harbor eligibility. No official filing performed.
3. Confirm actual sales jurisdictions, not just a globally reachable website.
   Puerto Rico consumer protection, DACO practice/advertising requirements,
   applicable federal law, and any state/foreign privacy/renewal obligations need
   jurisdiction-specific review. Do not assert all U.S. state laws or GDPR apply
   automatically; likewise do not assume they cannot apply.
4. Approve the 18+ consumer-account policy and staff process for suspected minors.
   COPPA generally concerns child-directed services and actual knowledge of under-13
   users; 18+ is a product choice, not a universal COPPA threshold. Adult attestation
   is not identity/age verification and cannot override actual knowledge. No ID
   collection is introduced. Guardian reports need careful verification/minimization.
5. Confirm retention purposes and operational deletion/backups schedules based on
   records and obligations; no unsupported fixed period should be promised publicly.
6. Marketing: verify each sender's real postal address, recipient authorization and
   suppression. Do not enable future Marketplace campaigns, sponsored placements,
   public ratings or tracking until dedicated controls and legal review exist.
7. Check recurring-billing disclosures and renewal notices against jurisdictions
   actually served. A 2024 FTC click-to-cancel amendment should not be represented
   as a currently operative universal requirement without checking litigation and
   newer rulemaking. Existing online cancellation is retained as a product control.

## Consumer quick guide / Guía rápida de consumidores

Browse without registering. An optional adult account saves private favorites and
municipality preferences; it is separate from a business portal account. Review the
business's price, policies and appointment terms before paying. Stripe QR opens
Stripe Checkout; a pending screen is not an invitation to pay again. Ask the cashier
to check the existing transaction. Share receipt links only with intended recipients.
Remove the Marketplace profile/favorites in My Account or contact support for help.

Navega sin registrarte. La cuenta opcional para adultos guarda favoritos privados y
municipio; es distinta del portal del negocio. Revisa precios, políticas y condiciones
de la cita antes de pagar. Stripe QR abre Stripe Checkout; una pantalla pendiente no
significa que debas pagar otra vez. Pide al cajero verificar la transacción existente.
Comparte enlaces de recibo solo con destinatarios previstos. Elimina perfil/favoritos
en Mi cuenta o solicita ayuda a soporte.

## Business quick guide / Guía rápida de negocios

Publish only content you own or are licensed to use, including AI assets. Keep
prices, taxes, refund/deposit rules and public contact details accurate. Protect
customer screens from bystanders, revoke uncontrolled devices and verify pending
payments before a retry. Manual ATH records your confirmation, not provider proof.
Optional review consent does not authorize arbitrary campaigns; honor unsubscribe
without suppressing transactional receipts or booking/security messages.

Publica contenido propio o autorizado, incluidos recursos IA. Mantén correctos precios,
impuestos, reembolsos/depósitos y contactos públicos. Protege pantallas de terceros,
revoca dispositivos fuera de control y verifica pagos pendientes antes de reintentar.
ATH manual registra tu confirmación, no una prueba del proveedor. Aceptar emails
opcionales de reseñas no autoriza cualquier campaña; respeta bajas sin cancelar
recibos ni avisos transaccionales de reservas/seguridad.
