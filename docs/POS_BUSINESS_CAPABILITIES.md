# POS business profiles and module settings

Client Admin → Business settings / Ajustes del negocio → POS · Business operations / Operación del negocio. Choose Automatic or Restaurant, Barbershop, Beauty salon, Retail, Workshop or General. Save explicitly. The two module controls enable Cashier POS and Customer Display independently. Existing Self-service kiosk and Workforce controls remain directly below; no duplicate activation or employee binding flow is introduced.

Automatic uses the existing category (or template category when category is absent), with normalized Spanish accents. The business name never classifies its capabilities. Active catalog records determine products, services, appointment links and inventory support. Barber/salon/workshop presentations put services first; workshop product labels include parts; restaurants use Menu. POS and kiosk share the same pure profile resolver and bilingual copy. Contextual kitchen/professional links point to existing panels and require existing role permissions. A manual presentation override does not change settlement rules or pretend a retail checkout generates kitchen queues. Existing kitchen records remain accessible.

## Storage and safety

No database, migration, catalog duplication or business data rewrite. The existing tenant kiosk configuration in `clientCommerceStore` (`siteId/pos-kiosk/config.json`) gains an `operations` object. Kiosk enablement and device registration remain independent and are preserved during concurrent writes. Legacy cashier and display behavior defaults to enabled; kiosk/workforce retain their existing settings, including disabled defaults. No real business configuration is changed by deploying this code.

`client-pos-settings` authenticates the existing portal identity. All current business roles can read the redacted settings; writes require the same tenant's actual owner/manager membership and settings capability. Arbitrary site, employee, permission, time and amount fields cannot grant authority. Same-origin POST checks, bounded payloads, strong ETag writes and expected operation revisions prevent CSRF and stale administrator overwrites. Audit events retain server time, actor, prior and next settings; at 2,000 events writes fail closed pending archival. Private audit and employee/payment fields are not returned by the settings or kiosk DTOs.

Cashier disablement is enforced before creating direct sales, idempotent sale markers, Stripe checkout or Terminal tokens/intents. Already completed identical attempts may return their original response. Attempt status, order reconciliation, receipts, provider webhooks and settlement remain available. A request already admitted before disablement can finish; the toggle does not cancel/refund accepted orders or provider sessions. Pending attempts remain accessible in POS even when navigation normally hides the disabled module. Customer Display disablement blocks create, pair, sync and public reads; explicit revocation remains allowed. Re-enabling resumes unexpired, unrevoked display sessions. Kiosk checkout is independent of cashier/display flags.
